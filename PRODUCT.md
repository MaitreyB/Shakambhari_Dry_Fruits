# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Both everyday consumers buying for health and daily snacking, and premium gifting clients looking for elegant options for festivals, weddings, or corporate events.

## Product Purpose
To offer a handpicked selection of premium quality dry fruits, nuts, and exotic foods. Success means serving both daily nutritional needs and special occasion gifting through a seamless shopping experience.

## Positioning
Shakambhari provides high-quality dry fruits at competitive, wholesale prices, distinguishing itself by making premium products accessible.

## Operating Context
Visitors browse the catalog on web browsers (desktop and mobile) to explore individual products or curated gifting options. They use a cart sidebar for managing their selections before checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- The web app supports product listings, a dedicated gifting section, and a sliding cart sidebar.
- No established constraints on extending functionality, provided the core shopping flow remains intact.

## Brand Commitments
- Brand Name: Shakambhari Dry Fruits and Foods.
- The existing logo and core brand assets must be preserved.
- Core product categories (Products, Gifting) must remain central to the experience.

## Evidence on Hand
- A working React + Vite frontend with `ProductSection`, `GiftingSection`, and `CartSidebar` components.
- An existing logo asset referenced in the HTML (`/assets/Shakambhari Dry Fruits and Foods.jpeg`).

## Product Principles
1. **Accessible Premium:** Present products to reflect their high quality while emphasizing competitive, wholesale pricing.
2. **Dual Purpose:** The interface must elegantly serve both the quick, everyday buyer and the thoughtful, high-volume gifter.
3. **Brand Continuity:** Honor the established Shakambhari name, logo, and categorical structure to maintain customer trust.
